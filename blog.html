Criminal Law

What to prepare before consulting a lawyer for bail

Bring the FIR or complaint details, sections invoked, arrest status, court stage, prior orders if any, and a concise timeline. Quick access to accurate facts helps counsel evaluate urgency, forum, and documentation needed for the application.

Civil Litigation

Why documentation matters in civil and property disputes

Agreements, receipts, notices, title papers, communications, and dates often define the strength of a civil dispute. A chronological file helps identify limitation issues, reliefs, and whether negotiation or litigation is the better path.

Consumer Protection

When can a consumer complaint be considered?

Consumer grievances may arise from deficiency in service, defective goods, misleading promises, or unfair trade practices. Preserve invoices, contracts, emails, screenshots, service records, and earlier complaints before seeking legal guidance.

Many commercial contracts prescribe arbitration before court proceedings. Reviewing the clause early helps determine seat, venue, procedure, notice requirements, and the appointment mechanism for the arbitrator.
